## Sprint Ceremony Insights

### Daily Scrum

During our Daily Scrums, we discussed what each team member had completed, what we were currently working on, and whether we had any blockers.

Our team was divided into two main areas during this sprint. Eric and Hung were responsible for the backend, while Trung and Albaraae focused on the frontend using React. Since the frontend and backend were developed separately during this sprint, the Daily Scrum helped us keep track of the overall progress of the project.

The Daily Scrums helped us:

- **Communicate progress:** Each team member briefly explained what they had completed and what they planned to work on next.
- **Identify blockers:** We could mention technical problems or difficulties early and discuss possible solutions with the team.
- **Coordinate work:** Even though the frontend and backend were developed separately, sharing our progress helped us understand what the other team members were working on, so that it would be easy to merge both ends later.
- **Adapt the plan:** When a task took longer than expected or a problem occurred, we could adjust our priorities and decide what needed to be done next.

Overall, the Daily Scrum helped us stay organized and aware of the team's progress throughout the sprint.

### Sprint Review

During this sprint, our team made progress on both the frontend and backend of our website.

For the frontend, Trung and Albaraae worked with React to create the initial structure and user interface of the website. They created and organized React components and worked on the layout and pages needed for the website. The goal was to establish a clear foundation that the team can continue developing in the future sprint.

For the backend, Eric and Hung worked on the server-side part of the project. They set up and developed the backend structure and worked on the functionality needed for the application.

During the Sprint Review, we demonstrated the work completed by showing the current state of the frontend and the progress made on the backend. This allowed the team to review what had been accomplished during the sprint and identify what still needs to be developed.

The main result of this sprint was that we established the basic foundation of both the frontend and backend. The frontend has a working React structure and user interface, while the backend has its initial server-side structure and functionality. These will provide the foundation for the development planned for the next sprint.

### Sprint Retrospective – 4Ls

#### Liked

- We liked having clear responsibilities within the team, with some members focusing on the frontend and others on the backend.
- We enjoyed working with React and building the user interface for the website.
- We also enjoyed working on the backend and developing the server-side functionality of the project.
- We liked seeing the overall project gradually take shape as both the frontend and backend progressed.
- We enjoyed being able to work independently on our assigned parts while still working together as a team.
- We liked having regular Daily Scrums because they helped everyone stay informed about the team's progress.

#### Learned

- We learned more about developing a web application using React and backend technologies.
- We learned how to structure frontend components and backend code in a more organized way.
- We learned more about working with Git and managing our changes as a team.
- We learned that dividing a large project into smaller responsibilities makes it easier to manage.
- We learned that regular communication is important even when team members are working on different parts of the project.
- We learned more about planning and estimating development tasks and how unexpected technical issues can affect our schedule.

#### Lacked

- We sometimes lacked enough time to fully polish both the frontend and backend features.
- Some tasks could have been defined more clearly before we started working on them.
- We could have spent more time planning the structure of both the frontend and backend before implementation.
- We lacked enough time for extensive testing and fixing smaller issues.
- Sometimes it was difficult to estimate how long certain frontend and backend tasks would take.
- We could have had more time to review our work as a team and make improvements before the end of the sprint.

#### Longed For

- We would have liked to have more time to improve the visual design and user experience of the frontend.
- We would have liked to have more time to improve and test the backend functionality.
- We would have liked to have clearer task requirements and expectations from the beginning of the sprint.
- We would have liked to have more time for testing and fixing smaller issues across the project.
- We would have liked to have more time to explore different technical solutions and improve the quality of our implementation.
- We would like to have better planning and task estimation in the next sprint so that we can work more efficiently and complete more of our planned tasks.

### Overall Reflection

Overall, this sprint was a useful experience for our team. We made progress on both the frontend and backend parts of our website.

Working with separate frontend and backend responsibilities allowed each team member to focus on their assigned tasks. At the same time, the Scrum ceremonies helped us communicate our progress, identify problems, and stay organized.

We also learned that better planning, clearer task descriptions, and regular communication can help us work more efficiently in the future sprint. We can use these lessons to improve our teamwork and development process in the next sprint.
